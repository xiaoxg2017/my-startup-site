About Saddle Point Productions

We're a Las Vegas production support company. Our job is to make the companies we work for look good on show day.

What we do

Saddle Point Productions LLC provides production labor, LED video wall rental and support, staging, truss, and crank lifts to event production companies, AV providers, exhibit houses, and event teams working in Las Vegas.

We're intentionally not a full-service agency. We work as a subcontractor and local vendor. You bring the client and the creative, and we bring local hands and gear for the parts you need.

How we work

  • We confirm what we can cover in writing before you commit.
  • Our crew reports to your production lead and follows your show plan.
  • We tell you early when a request is outside our scope or a venue rule applies.
  • Every quote lists equipment, labor, and terms clearly.

Let's talk about your next Las Vegas show

Send the dates, venue, and what you need. We'll reply with what we can cover and a quote, usually within one business day.
