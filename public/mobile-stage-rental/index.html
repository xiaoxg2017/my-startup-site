Tell us about your event

Stage options depend on the site as much as the show.

  • Event date, setup window, and strike time
  • Site address and ground surface (asphalt, grass, sand)
  • Deck size and height you need, or the act you're staging
  • Whether you need a roof, wings, or a backdrop
  • Access for a vehicle and trailer to reach the stage spot

Permits and site rules

Outdoor events in Clark County and Las Vegas may need permits, and many sites have their own rules for wind, power, and vehicle access. Check with your venue or permit office early. We'll share the stage specs your permit application needs.

Questions we get

Can you add an LED wall to the stage?

Yes. We can quote the stage, an LED wall, and crew together.

What happens if it's windy?

Outdoor staging has wind limits. We'll go over the limits for the stage you're renting and agree on a plan before the event.
