We work for the production company, not around it

When your show lands in Las Vegas and you need extra hands or gear, we fill the gap. We take direction from your production manager or technical director and represent your company on site. We don't pitch your client.

Tell us the call times, positions, and equipment you need. We'll confirm what we can cover before you commit, and we'll tell you early if a request is outside what we can do well.

What to send for a fast quote

The more of this we have, the quicker we can confirm availability and price.

  • Show name, venue, and hall or room
  • Load-in, show, and load-out dates and times
  • Positions and headcount, or the equipment list
  • For LED: wall size, pixel pitch if specified, indoor or outdoor, and how it's supported (ground-stacked or flown)
  • Venue or show labor rules you already know about
  • On-site contact and how you want us to bill
