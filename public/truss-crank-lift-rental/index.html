Truss & crank lift rental

Truss, hardware, and crank lifts for ground-supported lighting, signage, and small LED walls, with crew to assemble them if you need it.

Request a quoteCall (702) 420-1941

What you can rent

Tell us what the truss needs to hold and we'll quote the parts.

  • Truss sections, corners, and base plates
  • Clamps, spigots, pins, and safety hardware
  • Crank lifts and stands for lighting and banners
  • Ground-supported structures for small LED walls

Load and safety

Every truss setup needs to stay within its rated load and span. Send what's going on the structure (fixtures, signage, LED panels, and their weights) and we'll check it before quoting.

We quote ground-supported systems. For anything flown from the venue ceiling, the venue's rigging rules apply. We'll help you coordinate.

Questions we get

Do you deliver and assemble?

Yes. You can rent the equipment alone or with a crew to assemble and strike it.

Ask when you request a quote and we'll confirm pickup times.
