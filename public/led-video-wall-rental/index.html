What's included

Every rental quote spells out exactly what's included. Typically:

  • LED panels and the frames or ground support to mount them
  • Video processing and the cabling to reach your source
  • Delivery, build, and strike at the venue
  • A technician on site to run the wall and handle problems
  • Spare modules for fast swaps during the show

Tell us about the wall

These details decide which panels and processing we quote.

  • Finished size, or the space you need to fill
  • Pixel pitch, if your designer has specified one, and the typical viewing distance
  • Indoor or outdoor, and how many hours a day it runs
  • Ground-stacked, on truss, or flown
  • Your content source and its resolution

We'll confirm the panel model, pitch, and quantity we can supply for your dates. Need a different spec than we stock? We'll say so instead of quoting something that doesn't fit.

Content that fits the wall

Content built to the wall's exact pixel dimensions looks sharper and avoids surprises at the show. We can send your designers a pixel map and content specs ahead of time. See LED content mapping and repair.

Questions we get

Do you rent LED walls without a technician?

We recommend a technician on any show. A wall that fails mid-session costs more than the labor. For simple setups, ask us and we'll look at it case by case.

Can you support another company's LED wall?

Yes. We can build, operate, and troubleshoot walls supplied by others. See LED mapping and repair.

What pixel pitch should I use?

It depends mostly on how close the audience will be and how big the wall is. Send us the room layout and viewing distance and we'll recommend options.
