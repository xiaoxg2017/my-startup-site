How to use this calendar

This calendar lists major trade shows and conventions in Las Vegas. Each entry shows the dates, venue, industry, and a link to the official event website. Open any show to see where its dates came from and when we last checked them.

Show dates are only part of the picture for production teams. Exhibitor move-in and move-out usually start before opening day and run past closing, and each show publishes its own schedule in its exhibitor manual. Big weeks also tighten the market for local crew, rental gear, and hotel rooms, so book early when your show overlaps another large event.

We update the list as organizers announce new dates. Currently listing 18 dated events. Spot an error or a missing show? Email xiao@saddlepointpro.com.

Common questions

Where are most Las Vegas trade shows held?

The largest shows use the Las Vegas Convention Center, the Venetian Expo, Caesars Forum, and Mandalay Bay Convention Center. Some, like CES and AWS re:Invent, spread across several venues at once.

Are these dates official?

Shows marked "Organizer-confirmed" were checked against the organizer's own website or documents. Shows marked "Confirm with organizer" come from independent listings. Either way, check the official site before you book.

Can Saddle Point Productions help with my show?

If you're producing, exhibiting at, or supplying a show in Las Vegas, we provide local production labor, LED video wall support, and staging equipment. Tell us about your show.
