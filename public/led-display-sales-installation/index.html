How a project runs

Every install goes through the same four steps.

  1. Site visit. We measure the space and check viewing distances, mounting surfaces, power, and where the signal will come from.
  2. Specification and quote. We recommend a pixel pitch, size, and brightness, then quote the display, mounting, processing, and labor separately so you can compare.
  3. Installation. We mount, wire, and set up the display, coordinating with your electrician or general contractor where needed.
  4. Handover and service. We show your team how to run it and agree on how service calls will work after install.

Questions we get

Do you handle permits?

For exterior signs and some electrical work, permits are required. We'll tell you what's likely to be needed and work with your contractor on it.

Can you replace or upgrade an existing display?

Yes. Send photos and the current model and we'll start with a site visit.
